The key difference from continuous-time is that the signal is only defined at specific, evenly spaced points in time (usually integers), rather than at _every_ instant. Think of it like a sampled signal.
We're still dealing with complex exponentials, but now in the discrete-time domain. The term "sequence" is often used interchangeably with "signal" in the discrete-time context.
