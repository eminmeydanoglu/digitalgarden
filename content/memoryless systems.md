A **system is memoryless** if its **output at any given time depends only on the input at that same time**. This means the system does not store any past values or anticipate future inputs.

A capacitor **stores electrical charge**, and its voltage depends on the accumulated past values of current. This storage aspect is why it is a system with memory.
