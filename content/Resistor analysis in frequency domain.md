Because `VR = RIR`, and `R` is a _real_ number (not complex), the phasor voltage `VR` and the phasor current `IR` have the _same angle_. This means that the sinusoidal voltage and current waveforms reach their peaks and zero crossings at the same time. They are "in phase."
