### Sign of the [[reactive power]]
in sinusoidal AC circuits, the **sign of the reactive power** Q tells you whether the load is **inductive** (Q>0)(Q>0) or **capacitive** (Q<0). The core reason is that inductors **cause current to lag** voltage (phase shift > 0), whereas capacitors **cause current to lead** voltage (phase shift <0).
sin is an odd function and Reactive power Q = V_rms * I_rms * sin(phase_shift)
