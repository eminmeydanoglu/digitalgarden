> q:: Any LTI system can be characterized completely in terms of its response to a unit impulse.

$x[n] = \sum_{k=-\infty}^{\infty} x[k]\delta[n-k]$
s

## [[sift]]ing property
This property states that any discrete-time signal, x[n], can be represented as a sum of scaled and shifted unit impulses (δ[n−k]).
