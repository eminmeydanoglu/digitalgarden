#rw
Reactance (X) is the measure of opposition that inductors and capacitors provide to alternating current (AC), arising specifically due to their ability to store and release energy in electric or magnetic fields.

Think of reactance as the "dynamic resistance," but here's the critical difference: unlike resistance, which dissipates energy as heat, reactance temporarily stores energy and returns it back to the circuit.

The reverse is [[susceptance]]
