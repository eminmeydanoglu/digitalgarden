### For CT, two basic things: 
1. As you increase the frequency (`ω₀`), the sinusoidal oscillation gets faster and faster, and each different frequency produces a _unique_ signal. There's no ambiguity.
2.  e^(jω₀t) is periodic for any value of ω₀. No matter what frequency you choose for a continuous-time complex exponential, it will always be periodic.
