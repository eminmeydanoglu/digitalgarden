2. In AC circuits, phase angle of impedance is 
		The phase angle $\theta_Z$ is defined as the angle between the **real axis** (resistance) and the impedance vector. From trigonometry:
		![[Pasted image 20250316161405.png]]

- **If** $\omega L > \frac{1}{\omega C}$ (**inductive dominance**), then $\theta_Z > 0$, meaning **current lags voltage**.
- **If** $\omega L < \frac{1}{\omega C}$ (**capacitive dominance**), then $\theta_Z < 0$, meaning **current leads voltage**.
- **At resonance** ($\omega = \frac{1}{\sqrt{LC}}$), we get $\theta_Z = 0$, meaning **current and voltage are in phase**.
