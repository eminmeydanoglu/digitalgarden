Now if ω1=ω2, those “cross-terms” become cosines at 0 hz or at 2w. The constant pieces from the cross-terms do **not** average out—they survive and alter the total DC power. In other words, the two sinusoids interfere (constructively or destructively), and you cannot separate the net power into a simple sum of “power of source A” plus “power of source B.”
