When you see a household supply labeled “220 V” (or “120 V,” “230 V,” etc.), that is always the **RMS** voltage of the AC sine wave.
Power Rating (100 W) Is Real Power at the RMS Voltage. The 100 W rating is the **real (average) power** the lamp dissipates at the rated RMS voltage.
