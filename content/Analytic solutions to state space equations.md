![[Pasted image 20250225160815.png]]zero-input response is the part of the system's output that is due **solely to the initial conditions** of the system, assuming there is **no external input** applied after time `t=0`. It is how the system would behave if `X_p(t)` is zero. zero-state response, the same logic.
If eigenvalue is less than zero, natural response goes to zero, system is "asymptoticcally stable".
