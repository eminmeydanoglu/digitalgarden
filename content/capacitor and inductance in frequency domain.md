### interestingly,
![[Pasted image 20250309153205.png]]

`∠Ic = ∠Vc + π/2`

- This means the _angle_ (or phase) of the current `Ic` is 90 degrees (π/2 radians) _ahead_ of the angle (phase) of the voltage `Vc`.

`∠VL = ∠IL + π/2`
- The angle of the voltage is 90 ahead of the current.
- **Key Point:** "The current lags the voltage by 90°." This is the key characteristic of an inductor in an AC circuit. Because voltage leads, it is also correct to say current lags.
